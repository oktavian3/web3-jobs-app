## 1. Homepage

### Headline

**Understand the work before you chase the title.**

### Supporting copy

KRAFT helps you compare real Web3 roles, test possible fit, build proof-of-work, prepare for interviews, and find safer places to apply.

### Body copy

#### Start with the problem you have
You do not need to know the perfect title before using KRAFT. Start by browsing roles when you already have a direction, take the Skill Check when several paths look plausible, or open Get Hired when you are already applying.

#### What you can do here
Read detailed role guides. Compare overlapping titles. Learn the vocabulary that appears in job descriptions. Follow practical roadmaps. Build portfolio evidence. Practice interviews. Review compensation with the right caveats. Use curated job sources without confusing a directory with a guarantee.

#### How the pieces connect
A role guide explains the work. A roadmap sequences the prerequisites. Learn Web3 provides targeted resources. A portfolio brief turns learning into evidence. Interview Prep shows what companies may test. Job Boards points to external sources. Methodology explains where KRAFT is confident and where it is not.

#### What KRAFT does not do
KRAFT does not guarantee employment, rank human potential, predict compensation, or treat public activity as a definitive assessment. The goal is clearer decisions and stronger evidence.

### Calls to action

- Find a role.
- Start the Skill Check.

## 2. Roles Directory

### Headline

**Find the role closest to the work you want to do.**

### Supporting copy

Browse by function, technical depth, work style, entry expectations, and employment model. Similar titles include boundary notes so you can see where one role stops and another begins.

### Body copy

#### How to use the directory
Start with a lane, then compare two or three roles. Read the boundary note before choosing. A role that sounds familiar may still have very different decision rights, deliverables, and hiring evidence.

#### Filters that matter
Filter by lane, technical depth, public-facing versus internal work, analytical versus execution-heavy work, typical entry level, and employment model. KRAFT does not label whole roles as simply easy or hard.

#### Titles are inconsistent
Web3 teams often publish similar work under different titles. Each page includes alternative market titles, but KRAFT uses one consistent taxonomy so comparisons and learning paths stay stable.

#### Do not choose from salary alone
Salary evidence is uneven and geography-sensitive. Use role fit, prerequisite depth, proof requirements, and employment model before reading compensation context.

- Compare selected roles.

## 11. Get Hired

### Headline

**Build an application around evidence.**

### Supporting copy

A strong application makes the work visible. It does not rely on vague passion, follower count, or a generic crypto résumé.

### Body copy

#### Choose the right target
Define the role, level, location, employment model, and evidence before applying.

#### Research the team
Read the product, documentation, recent launches, incidents, funding or business model, and current job scope.

#### Package proof
Use an ATS-safe résumé for application portals. Keep creative work in a portfolio. Show context, decisions, ownership, constraints, and outcomes.

#### Apply with relevance
Tailor the opening, evidence, and questions. Do not rewrite your entire identity for each listing.

#### Use outreach carefully
Contact a relevant person with one clear reason, one useful proof link, and no mass-DM behavior.

#### Handle trial tasks
Confirm scope, time, ownership, compensation, confidentiality, and whether the work resembles unpaid production.

#### Prepare for interviews
Use role-specific questions, evidence, incidents, trade-offs, and honest gaps.

#### Review offers
Separate cash, token, equity, bonus, commission, vesting, location, employment status, and tax or legal questions.

#### Stay safe
Verify domains, people, contracts, and wallet requests. Never share a seed phrase or pay to access a job application.

### Calls to action

## 19. Methodology

### Headline

**How KRAFT turns messy job titles into usable role guides.**

### Supporting copy

Role pages combine current first-party hiring material, protocol documentation, official team material, and reputable industry evidence. Claims are labelled by strength.

### Body copy

#### Source hierarchy
Current official job listings and company career pages; protocol or product documentation; first-party team material; reputable industry reports; job-board aggregates as secondary context.

#### Claim labels
Verified fact, common industry pattern, recommendation, exception, and needs human verification.

#### Taxonomy decisions
KRAFT uses stable role titles for navigation. Alternative market titles remain searchable. Merges and splits are based on ownership, deliverables, and hiring evidence.

#### Review process
Record source, date, geography, role scope, reviewer, and confidence. Correct material errors visibly.

#### Limitations
Public job listings skew toward funded companies, some work is recruited privately, and titles vary by team.

## 20. Salary Methodology

### Headline

**Salary context without fake precision.**

### Supporting copy

Web3 compensation changes by geography, level, employment model, company stage, token or equity, and market cycle. KRAFT shows a number only when the evidence can support it.

### Body copy

#### Evidence tiers
Direct role-specific evidence; adjacent-role evidence; broad Web3 market evidence; unverified estimate.

#### Confidence
High: several matching current listings. Medium: limited direct evidence plus strong adjacent context. Low: mostly adjacent or mixed markets. Very low: no reliable role-specific evidence.

#### Display rules
High and medium may show numeric ranges with caveats. Low usually shows evidence context. Very low states that a reliable range is unavailable.

#### Compensation components
Separate base cash, bonus, commission, equity, token, vesting, contractor rate, retainer, bounty, royalty, grant, and creator revenue.

#### What never happens
No fabricated range for layout consistency. No conversion of US remote pay into a local promise. No use of validator rewards, creator sales, or trading bonuses as standard salary.
