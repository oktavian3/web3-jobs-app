# KRAFT Shared Content Blocks

These blocks are intentionally reusable. The text under **Public block** may appear on the website. Placement and eligibility notes are implementation guidance and must not be rendered as part of the block.

## SB-01 — Role titles vary

**Public block**

Web3 teams do not use titles consistently. KRAFT keeps one canonical title for navigation and comparison, then lists the alternative titles commonly used in job listings. Always compare the responsibilities, decision rights, and proof requirements before assuming two titles describe the same job.

**Use on:** Roles Directory, role comparison, Methodology.

---

## SB-02 — Compensation evidence legend

**Public block**

KRAFT separates compensation evidence into four tiers:

- **Direct:** evidence from the same or a materially equivalent role.
- **Adjacent:** evidence from a neighbouring occupation, used only for context.
- **Broad market:** category-level Web3 or labour-market evidence.
- **Unverified:** estimates without enough source or methodology detail.

Confidence reflects the quality and comparability of the evidence, not the value or legitimacy of the role.

**Use on:** Salary Methodology and expandable help beside role compensation.

---

## SB-03 — No reliable salary range

**Public block**

KRAFT did not find a reliable role-specific range that meets the evidence standard. Compensation may still exist through salary, contract fees, retainers, grants, commissions, token or equity packages, creator revenue, or business economics. These models are described separately rather than compressed into an invented number.

**Use on:** Low and very-low confidence compensation sections.

---

## SB-04 — Compensation variables

**Public block**

Compensation can change materially by geography, seniority, employment model, company stage, market cycle, and the mix of cash, bonus, commission, equity, token, vesting, royalties, or fees. A published range is useful only when those dimensions match the role being considered.

**Use on:** Salary Methodology and role pages with numeric evidence. Do not repeat on every role when the contextual link is sufficient.

---

## SB-05 — Employment models are not interchangeable

**Public block**

Full-time employment, contracting, freelancing, contributor work, ambassador programmes, creator deals, grants, validator businesses, and agency work carry different scope, protections, costs, and compensation. KRAFT keeps these models separate even when the work looks similar.

**Use on:** Roles Directory, Get Hired, compensation help.

---

## SB-06 — Portfolio ownership and confidentiality

**Public block**

A strong portfolio makes ownership visible. Explain the problem, available evidence, constraints, decisions, deliverable, and result. State what you owned and what belonged to other people. Redact private user, company, wallet, security, legal, or compensation information instead of publishing it for proof.

**Use on:** Portfolio index, portfolio briefs, role proof sections.

---

## SB-07 — Metrics need context

**Public block**

A metric needs a definition, source, time window, and decision. Token incentives, launches, incidents, paid distribution, market volatility, and attribution gaps can move activity without proving durable performance. Pair volume with quality, correctness, risk, retention, or downstream outcomes.

**Use on:** Methodology and a help panel linked from role success sections.

---

## SB-08 — Claim labels

**Public block**

- **Verified fact:** supported by a current source for the stated scope.
- **Common industry pattern:** repeated across credible examples but not universal.
- **Recommendation:** KRAFT editorial guidance.
- **Exception:** a material case where the common pattern does not apply.
- **Needs human verification:** not ready to publish as fact.

**Use on:** Methodology, source drawers, editorial previews. The unresolved label should not appear on public pages unless the uncertainty itself is useful and clearly explained.

---

## SB-09 — Job-board safety

**Public block**

A listing in KRAFT is not a guarantee that the role remains open or that the employer is trustworthy. Continue through the employer's official domain, verify the recruiter and contract, and never send a seed phrase, private key, wallet signature, or payment to apply for a job.

**Use on:** Job Boards, Get Hired, empty listing states.

---

## SB-10 — Skill Check limitations

**Public block**

The Skill Check suggests roles worth investigating. It does not measure intelligence, personality, aptitude, employability, hiring suitability, compensation value, or definitive career fit. Results are based on the answers provided and may miss experience that was not captured.

**Use on:** Skill Check introduction and results.

---

## SB-11 — X Profile Role Matcher limitations

**Public block**

The matcher looks only at visible public profile evidence and returns a possible role fit with low, medium, or high confidence. Public signals can be incomplete, performative, outdated, or unrelated to professional ownership. The result is not an assessment of employability, aptitude, intelligence, personality, hiring suitability, or compensation value.

**Use on:** Experimental matcher introduction and results. Never promote as a primary assessment.

---

## SB-12 — Stateless session

**Public block**

This version is stateless. Inputs are used to produce the current result and are not saved as a profile or result history. Closing or resetting the session removes the current working state.

**Use on:** Skill Check, X Matcher, Privacy, relevant error states. Publish only after the deployed data flow confirms the claim.

---

## SB-13 — Creator compensation

**Public block**

Creator income is not a standard salary band. Primary sales, royalties, commissions, grants, brand collaborations, licensing, and studio or agency contracts have different fees, rights, costs, and volatility. Gross sales should not be presented as personal income.

**Use on:** NFT Artist / Generative Artist, Content Creator where relevant, Salary Methodology.

---

## SB-14 — Validator economics

**Public block**

Validator revenue is business economics, not employee salary. Commission, delegated stake, self-stake, infrastructure cost, token price, uptime, slashing exposure, and protocol rules all affect the result. Chain-specific numbers require current official documentation.

**Use on:** Node Operator / Validator and Salary Methodology.

---

## SB-15 — Market-making compensation and performance

**Public block**

Market-making compensation may combine base salary, guaranteed or discretionary bonus, PnL-linked pay, token or equity, and firm-level economics. Gross PnL or volume is not meaningful without capital usage, inventory risk, drawdown, fees, adverse selection, and the agreed risk limits.

**Use on:** Market Maker and Salary Methodology.

---

## SB-16 — Legal and compliance boundary

**Public block**

KRAFT explains common work patterns and hiring evidence. It does not provide legal, regulatory, tax, or compliance advice. Requirements vary by jurisdiction, product, entity structure, customer type, and current law.

**Use on:** Web3 Legal / Compliance, Disclaimers, Salary Methodology where tax is discussed.

---

## SB-17 — Corrections and review dates

**Public block**

Sources, job listings, tools, protocol mechanics, and compensation evidence can change. Each source-linked record includes a review date and status. Material corrections should update the claim, preserve the reason for the change, and record the next review date.

**Use on:** Methodology and source register.
