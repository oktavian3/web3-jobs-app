# KRAFT Master Taxonomy

**Canonical role count:** 42  
**Career lanes:** 8  
**Product definition:** KRAFT is a Web3 career learning centre. It is not a course platform, recruitment agency, job guarantee, or salary calculator.

## Taxonomy rules

1. Canonical titles are editorial product decisions, not claims that the market uses one universal title.
2. Alternative market titles appear on every role page and in search metadata.
3. Similar roles remain separate when they own materially different decisions, deliverables, or hiring evidence.
4. Legacy routes must redirect. Do not delete old URLs and create dead links.
5. A participation model such as contributor, contractor, ambassador, validator business, or independent creator is not automatically an employee job.
6. Salary confidence does not determine whether a role is valid. It determines how compensation evidence may be presented.
7. The X Profile Role Matcher is an experimental secondary feature and must not redefine the taxonomy.

## Boundary decisions

### Community roles
- **Community Moderator** owns frontline safety, routine answers, enforcement, and escalation.
- **Community Manager** owns community strategy, programming, feedback loops, health reporting, and moderator systems.
- **Ambassador Manager** owns a structured advocate programme: recruitment, onboarding, task design, quality, rewards, and disputes.
- **Creator & Ambassador Partner** owns individual or campaign relationships with creators and ambassadors, including briefs, rights, disclosure, and performance review.
- **Growth Manager** owns growth diagnosis and experiments across acquisition, activation, retention, and referral.
- **Ecosystem Partnerships Manager** owns ecosystem-level integrations and relationships.
- **Partnerships Manager** owns company-level commercial or distribution partnerships.

### Content roles
- **Content Creator** creates original media.
- **Social Media Manager** owns channel planning, publishing governance, copy operations, and performance reporting.
- **Research Writer** produces sourced analysis with methodology and limitations.
- **Crypto Journalist** reports news and investigations under editorial standards.
- **Product Marketing Manager** owns product positioning, launch messaging, audience, and enablement.

### Product and operations roles
- **Product Manager** owns product decisions and trade-offs.
- **Product Operations** owns the operating system around feedback, launches, decisions, and retrospectives.
- **Operations Assistant** provides scoped administrative and coordination support.
- **Operations Associate** owns recurring cross-functional programmes and operating workflows.
- **Customer Support Specialist** owns safe case resolution and support insight.
- **Technical Writer** owns documentation architecture and task-based technical content.

### Research and economics roles
- **Onchain Data Analyst** defines and analyses blockchain metrics, cohorts, wallets, and protocol behavior.
- **DeFi Analyst** interprets DeFi protocols, markets, and risk.
- **Protocol Researcher** studies mechanism, architecture, governance, and protocol assumptions.
- **Tokenomics Analyst** evaluates an existing or proposed token model.
- **Tokenomics Designer** designs incentives, allocation, emissions, governance mechanics, and economic architecture.
- **Ecosystem Researcher** verifies programmes, opportunities, and ecosystem developments without guaranteeing rewards.

### Technical roles
- **Smart Contract Developer** writes application or protocol contracts.
- **Protocol Engineer** builds the core protocol or client layer.
- **Backend Engineer** builds services, APIs, indexers, and data systems.
- **Frontend Web3 Developer** builds wallet-integrated user interfaces.
- **Smart Contract Auditor** independently reviews security and adversarial failure modes.
- **Node Operator / Validator** runs chain infrastructure and operations.
- **Developer Relations** drives developer adoption through code, docs, examples, support, and education.
- **ZK Engineer / Cryptography Researcher** stays one page with two tracks:
  - ZK Engineer: implementation, circuits, proving systems, tooling, performance, integration.
  - Cryptography Researcher: primitives, proof systems, formal security arguments, new constructions, research.
  - Shared foundation: advanced mathematics, algorithms, cryptography, and proof-system literacy.
  - Hiring difference: engineers need stronger systems and implementation evidence; researchers often need deeper formal or publication evidence.

### Governance, legal, people, and education
- **Governance Coordinator** operates governance processes and execution tracking.
- **Grant Writer** remains standalone even when the work is freelance, fractional, contract-based, or combined with ecosystem and operations.
- **Web3 Educator / Curriculum Builder** contains two tracks:
  - Developer Education
  - User / Community Education
- **Web3 Legal / Compliance** and **Web3 HR / Talent Acquisition** require domain-specific professional evidence and must not be treated as beginner roles by default.

### Creative and finance-adjacent
- **Web3 Product Designer** owns product interaction, usability, and Web3 states.
- **Brand Designer** owns visual identity and campaign systems.
- **Motion Designer** owns animation and motion systems.
- **NFT Artist / Generative Artist** uses a creator compensation model, not a default employee salary model.
- **Market Maker** remains canonical. Alternative listing titles include Quant Trader, Algorithmic Trader, and Liquidity Provider.

## Redirect behavior

- Use permanent redirects only after the canonical destination is live and content-complete.
- Preserve query parameters only when they do not expose personal or unsafe data.
- Update internal links, canonical tags, sitemap entries, structured data, and portfolio references.
- Legacy combined Brand / Motion pages must show a visible note explaining that the role was split.
- Do not redirect `/posts` to a fake archive. Keep it hidden until real editorial inventory exists.

## Taxonomy notes requiring future review

- Ambassador Manager title usage remains inconsistent. Keep the title and include the approved alternative market titles.
- Grant Writer remains a valid role even though the employment market is often fractional or contract-based.
- Market Maker hiring frequently appears under trading titles. Search and SEO must include those alternatives.
- ZK Engineer and Cryptography Researcher share a page for now. Split only if future content volume and hiring evidence justify independent pages.
- Do not add Founder’s Associate in this phase.
